fenceline

The Valley

take the long way.

road

the road rises, bends, then disappears behind a hill.

grass

green all the way to the fence.

greener on the other side too.

sheep

small marks in the paddock until one of them moves.

Animals

closer to the road.
slower about everything.

vines

the rows begin suddenly and hold the slope in place.

Kangaroos

heads down in the long grass.

one looks up when you pass.

shade

old trees lean over the road as if it came later.

late light

the fences keep going.

so do you.

another bend.
